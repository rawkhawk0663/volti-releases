Volti is a sheet music reader for musicians, made for the **Surface with pen** on Windows. It also works on any Windows device with mouse and keyboard. An Android version is in the works.

Volti is available in **English and German** (⚙ → *Appearance* → *Sprache · Language*). Volti needs **no account**, collects **no data** and works **offline**. The only time Volti goes online is to look for updates.

## Installation (Windows 10 and 11)

1. Open the **[latest version](../../releases/latest)**.
2. Download **`Volti-Setup.exe`** and run it.
3. The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Tap **More info → Run anyway**. You see this message because Volti isn't signed with a paid certificate.
4. Volti now appears in the Start menu.

Installing doesn't need administrator rights. Uninstall it from the Windows settings like any other app. Your music stays where it is.

**Without installing:** download `Volti-Windows.zip` instead, unzip it into a folder and run `Volti.exe`.

**Updates:** Volti checks for new versions on its own when it starts. Under ⚙ → *About Volti* → *Check for updates* you install them with one tap, and Volti then restarts.

## Features

### Library
- **Import PDFs**, one at a time or a whole folder. Volti keeps its own copy and leaves your originals untouched.
- **Folders** for projects, services or ensembles, including subfolders.
- **Details** for every piece: title, composer, **key**, tempo, time signature, genre, tags and notes. The key is clearly visible everywhere, and versions of the same song in different keys are listed together.
- **Search** by title, composer, key, genre and tags.
- **Select several pieces** to move, delete, add to a setlist or share as PDF in one go.

### Setlists
- Put together the songs for a service, rehearsal or concert, and drag them into order.
- While playing, a setlist behaves like **one single book**: after the last page of a song, the next song follows right away.
- **Note sheets**: blank pages inside the setlist, e.g. for the running order and cues.
- **Notes for this setlist only**: layers you create inside a setlist appear only there. Notes from the library appear everywhere.
- Folders and search for setlists.

### Playing
- **Turn pages** by tapping the right or left edge, by swiping, with a pedal (Bluetooth, e.g. iRig BlueTurn), with the arrow keys or the space bar.
- **Two pages side by side** in landscape. Each turn moves exactly one page, so you always see every transition.
- **Half page turns**: the top half changes first, so you never turn into the unknown.
- **Zoom** with two fingers, and the music stays sharp.
- **Jump links** for repeats, D.S. and coda: tap and Volti jumps there.
- **Bookmarks**, also for a range of pages, e.g. one song in a songbook.
- **Continuous scroll**: the staves are lined up across the full width so you can scroll through them.
- **Metronome** with a pleasant click. It picks up the tempo and time signature of every song.

### Writing with the pen
- **Pen, highlighter and eraser**, including the eraser end of the Surface Pen. The highlighter always stays beneath the notes.
- **Fingers never draw.** Your hand can rest on the screen while you write.
- **Text notes** with three fonts, colours and background. Inside a note you can make single words bold, italic, underlined or coloured.
- **Stamps** with more than a hundred music symbols in groups (dynamics, articulation, strings, winds, keys, guitar …), plus **your own stamps** from images.
- **Shapes**: line, arrow, rectangle, ellipse, crescendo, decrescendo, slur and staff lines.
- **Ruler** for straight lines, moved and rotated with your fingers.
- **Lasso** to move, resize, copy and recolour, plus a **move tool**.
- **Copy bars**: cut out bars including your notes and paste them elsewhere, even into another piece.
- **Layers and groups**, e.g. separate ones for fingerings and rehearsal notes, each of which you can show or hide.
- **Blank pages** inside a piece.
- **Favourites** for your preferred pen settings.
- Everything can be **undone**.

### Pages and export
- **Arrange pages**: change the order, hide or repeat pages, rotate, crop and straighten crooked scans. The PDF itself is never changed.
- **Share or print as PDF**, with all your notes if you like, even a whole setlist in one file.

### Sync and backup
- **Sync** between your devices: directly via **Dropbox** or **Google Drive**, or through a folder that a cloud program keeps in sync (e.g. Proton Drive). Notes from two devices are merged, never overwritten.
- **Automatic backup** every week, plus manual backups. Restoring a backup never deletes anything.

## Your data

Volti keeps everything on your device, in the folder `%APPDATA%\Volti`. ⚙ → *Open data folder* takes you there. Your PDFs are never changed: notes, page order and cropping are stored in a small extra file next to each copy.

## Reporting problems

Under ⚙ → *About Volti* → *Error log* you can see technical error messages. Use *Copy* to pass them on. They contain no music and no personal content.
